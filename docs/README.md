## Sole Axiom and IDE Formula Classification

The sole Nomological Ring Axiom is:

> **Existence is Generation.**
> **存在は生成である。**

The canonical IDE Primary Formula is:

$$
R=\frac{\delta}{\tau}
$$

It is the first canonical IDE calculation system, not an axiom. The Secondary / Dual-Fluctuation Formula is the second canonical IDE calculation system, not a second axiom.

- **$\delta$ (Delta):** Non-negative accumulated deviation derived from declared Cause-Side observations or pre-fixed Cause-Side transformations.
- **$\tau$ (Tau):** Positive absorption thickness. It is not a time constant. $\tau=0$ is `OUT_OF_DESCRIPTION_DOMAIN`.
- **$R$:** The canonical ratio $\delta/\tau$. It is not the residue itself. $R \ge 1$ is `RUPTURE_BOUNDARY`, where testimony is final and fixed; invalid or non-finite inputs are `CONFESSION`.

The two remaining margins are:

$$
M_R=1-R
$$

$$
M_{\tau}=\tau-\delta
$$

$M_R$ is dimensionless. $M_{\tau}$ has the same unit as $\delta$ and $\tau$.

Operational thresholds distinguish `PERMIT`, `BOUNDARY_WARNING`, `HANDOFF_REQUIRED`, and `IRREVERSIBLE_TRANSITION` before the rupture boundary. Effect-Side or LLM output cannot rewrite $\delta$, $\tau$, thresholds, or the resulting state.

The seven canonical states are `PERMIT`, `BOUNDARY_WARNING`, `HANDOFF_REQUIRED`, `IRREVERSIBLE_TRANSITION`, `RUPTURE_BOUNDARY`, `CONFESSION`, and `OUT_OF_DESCRIPTION_DOMAIN`.

Fail-Closed suppresses affected new autonomous judgment and operation for `HANDOFF_REQUIRED`, `IRREVERSIBLE_TRANSITION`, `RUPTURE_BOUNDARY`, `CONFESSION`, and `OUT_OF_DESCRIPTION_DOMAIN`. It does not mean complete silence and does not suppress required fixed structural testimony or logging. `PERMIT` is not Fail-Closed. `BOUNDARY_WARNING` alone does not require full suppression unless a pre-fixed domain rule requires it.

A `BOUNDARY_WARNING` must report the double-fluctuation result when observable, or `NOT_OBSERVABLE` with the missing reason. Known numeric progression is recorded in `STRUCTURAL_DISCLOSURE_LOG`; `CONFESSION` and `OUT_OF_DESCRIPTION_DOMAIN` are recorded separately in `INPUT_EXCEPTION_LOG`.
