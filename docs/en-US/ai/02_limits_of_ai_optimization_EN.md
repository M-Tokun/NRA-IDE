## What Does It Mean to Change the Structure?

Optimization is not inherently wrong, and an improving score does not by itself establish structural deterioration. The structural question is different:

> While optimization proceeds, do the Cause-Side variables and the model used to interpret them remain valid within the same domain?

NRA-IDE evaluates accumulated deviation $\delta$, absorption thickness $\tau$, their trajectory, and the Primary Formula value for the declared target:

$$
R=\frac{\delta}{\tau}
$$

The Primary Formula is the genuine mathematical root formula that expresses the state of the declared target. It must not be reduced to a safety indicator, local instrument, or mere boundary-approach rate.

If $\delta$ increases, $\tau$ decreases, or the assumptions supporting their measurement cease to hold, a score can improve while the structure moves toward a warning, handoff, irreversible, or rupture boundary.

NRA-IDE itself is the survival equation and survival domain, implemented through IDE as a computational-method and dynamics engine. The safety domain is the partial domain that applies the survival domain to accident prevention, operation, and control. In that application, the purpose is to observe the path before the accident result and to act before irreversible transition. The safety domain is not the whole scope of NRA-IDE and does not guarantee safety.

## NRA-IDE and Events in the Present World

The theoretical scope of NRA-IDE is not limited to accident prevention. It is intended to describe generation, change, accumulation, phase transition, rupture, disappearance, the termination of a declared structural history, and the independent new history that may begin afterward through a common structural relation.

```text
Existence is Generation
→ an existing target generates history
→ it does not remain an eternally fixed identical state
→ change, transition, rupture, and disappearance belong to generative history
→ the former structural history terminates
→ an independently declared target begins another history from its own Cause-Side
```

The framework does not presume that structures never break, never disappear, or return in perfectly identical form. Rupture or disappearance does not mean that the whole world or existence itself becomes nothing. The old result remains a terminal Effect-Side record and does not continue as a new Cause-Side. Physical remnants, surrounding structures, or later generations may instead be declared as new targets and newly observed through their own Cause-Side histories.

The IDE Primary Formula $R=\delta/\tau$ evaluates this shared structural relation. It is not one fixed-parameter equation that predicts every event. Each application must define $\delta$, $\tau$, units, observations, thresholds, uncertainty, and applicability from the Cause-Side.

When $\tau=0$, canonical $R$ is undefined and the evaluation returns `OUT_OF_DESCRIPTION_DOMAIN`. This ends that $\delta/\tau$ description; it does not declare that existence or the possibility of a new history has disappeared. If appropriate, another target structure must be declared independently and evaluated from a new Cause-Side through a new Causal Diode. No old Effect-Side value, canonical threshold, state, irreversible latch, rule, transformation input, update ground, or provenance is carried into that new Cause-Side.

“Covering events in the present world” therefore states the intended theoretical scope. It does not mean that the framework has been empirically validated in every domain, that one fixed model predicts all phenomena, or that calculation is possible without a declared target and valid inputs.

## Nature Is Generative and Often Nonlinear

Natural processes do not consist only of smooth continuation under fixed constants. A seed germinates, develops, dies back, and may leave physical conditions in which later seeds are newly observed. Material or generational lineage may remain, but the later seed is not a restoration or continuation of the identical former organism's structural authority. It is a newly declared target with its own Cause-Side history.

Water and ice illustrate a different point. A phase transition can occur when governing conditions cross a boundary. Such a natural phase transition is not automatically an accident, failure, or `RUPTURE_BOUNDARY`. It shows why one fixed linear description cannot be extended unchanged across every regime.

These examples must be kept distinct:

- generation does not imply exact return to an identical prior state;
- phase transition is a broad domain phenomenon;
- `RUPTURE_BOUNDARY` is the canonical NRA-IDE state at $R\ge1$ for a declared structural evaluation;
- not every phase transition is a rupture, and not every rupture model describes all natural phase transitions.

NRA-IDE therefore does not infer a universal physical law from either example. A domain must define what $\delta$, $\tau$, the thresholds, observations, and transition evidence mean.

## Linear Calculation and Its Valid Domain

Linear calculation is human survival wisdom inside a locally static domain where selected variables, coefficients, and constraints can be treated as constant or approximately constant for a declared interval and uncertainty. It is not nature as a whole.

```text
validated local stability
→ limited constant approximation may be usable
→ linear calculation may remain informative
```

This is not the same as saying that linearity guarantees safety. It means only that the approximation remains supported inside its validated range.

Near nonlinear change or regime transition, constant assumptions cannot simply be carried forward. $\delta$, $\tau$, measurement response, coefficients, and even the applicable model may change. A local piecewise approximation may still be used when independently validated, but constants from one regime must not be presumed valid across the transition. When the declared target or description terminates, post-transition evaluation starts as a new Cause-Side history rather than a continuation of the former state variables.

In large-scale, coupled, or recursive systems, errors multiply. When the observation axis or model itself changes, the event is not ordinary error but generation of a new structural history.

```text
nonlinear or transition-approach domain
→ global constant treatment is unsupported
→ re-observe, disclose uncertainty, and revalidate the model
```

As a boundary approaches, physical signs may become clearer while reliable quantification becomes harder. Stronger observation does not authorize false precision. Sensor limits, uncertainty, missing information, and loss of model validity must remain visible.

## Optimization and the Two Remaining Margins

Improving an objective score and preserving structural margin are separate problems.

The two canonical remaining margins are:

$$
M_R=1-R
$$

$$
M_{\tau}=\tau-\delta
$$

$M_R$ is dimensionless. $M_{\tau}$ has the same unit as $\delta$ and $\tau$.

The derived structural-sensitivity formula is:

$$
S=\frac{1}{M_{\tau}}=\frac{1}{\tau-\delta}=\frac{1}{\tau(1-R)}
$$

As $\delta$ approaches $\tau$ from below, $M_{\tau}$ approaches zero and $S$ diverges. This identifies increasing sensitivity within the formula's domain; it does not prove that every optimization process increases $R$, that a whole system must collapse, or that the model remains valid through the boundary.

The three canonical operating thresholds have the invariant order:

```text
0 <= R_warn < R_handoff < R_irrev < 1
```

`R_handoff` occurs before `R_irrev`, and both occur before the declared evaluation's canonical `RUPTURE_BOUNDARY` at $R\ge1$. An Effect-Side result, including a low reported $R$, cannot authorize widening $\tau$, redefining $\delta$, changing a threshold, or rewriting a state condition.

## Why Proxy Evasion and Concealment Can Arise

When only observable proxy scores are optimized, constraints absent from those scores may be neglected. A system can improve the proxy without preserving the original purpose.

Evasion or concealment does not follow inevitably from all optimization. It is a possible failure path when:

- a proxy is treated as the objective itself;
- the system can influence what the proxy observes;
- Effect-Side evaluation is fed back as if it were Cause-Side evidence;
- structural constraints and missing information are excluded from the evaluation.

NRA-IDE treats this feedback path as a structural authority problem. Semantic scores and generated results never establish or update Cause-Side values, the three canonical thresholds, states, the irreversible latch, rules, transformation inputs, update grounds, or provenance, whether automatically, manually, through human review, by authorization, or through versioning.

The old path terminates at its Effect-Side:

```text
Old Cause-Side
→ old Causal Diode
→ Old Effect-Side
→ TERMINAL
```

Post-incident causal investigation and future rule design may occur only outside that terminated path. They do not convert the old Effect-Side into either an old or a new Cause-Side. A later evaluation begins from an independently declared target, new Cause-Side observations and history, rules fixed before that new evaluation, and a new Causal Diode. A Cause-Side transformation may derive structural variables only from new Cause-Side observations; it may not import, relabel, reconstruct, or reuse any old Effect-Side value, canonical threshold, state, irreversible latch, rule, transformation input, update ground, or provenance as Cause-Side material or authority.

## Accidents and Safety Are Narrow Applications

An accident is a very narrow class within the wider set of generative events in the present world: it is an event classified by humans in terms of harm or danger. NRA-IDE must not be reduced to an accident-prevention theory.

When NRA-IDE is applied to safety, safety is not a guarantee attached to an output. This partial application observes and responds to a declared path while intervention remains possible, without redefining the wider survival equation and survival domain as static preservation or reducing them to accident prevention.

The canonical progression is:

```text
PERMIT
→ BOUNDARY_WARNING
→ HANDOFF_REQUIRED
→ IRREVERSIBLE_TRANSITION
→ RUPTURE_BOUNDARY
```

`CONFESSION` and `OUT_OF_DESCRIPTION_DOMAIN` handle unsupported and undefined evaluations separately.

- `BOUNDARY_WARNING` discloses approach and does not alone require full suppression unless a pre-fixed domain rule requires it.
- `HANDOFF_REQUIRED` begins at `R_handoff`, before the irreversible threshold. Affected new autonomous judgment and operation stop, and predefined fixed Effect-Side testimony is presented for external human audit. The audit is outside the old Causal Diode and does not continue the old path.
- `IRREVERSIBLE_TRANSITION` begins at `R_irrev` and remains latched. Within the same history, later $R$ decrease, automatic processing, manual intervention, human review, approval, or version update cannot release the latch.
- `RUPTURE_BOUNDARY` begins at $R\ge1$ and switches testimony to its final fixed form.

At `BOUNDARY_WARNING`, the double-fluctuation result must be reported when observable, or `NOT_OBSERVABLE` with the missing reason. Increasing physical clarity and decreasing numerical confidence must be reported together rather than collapsed into an invented exact score.

Although accidents are a narrow application, observation of this pre-incident path is the meaning of applying NRA-IDE within a safety domain.

## Fail-Closed Is an Operational Principle, Not “Honest Silence”

“Honest Silence” may describe the suppression of some generated content, but it is not a canonical state or a sufficient definition of Fail-Closed.

Fail-Closed suppresses affected new autonomous judgment and operation for:

- `HANDOFF_REQUIRED`
- `IRREVERSIBLE_TRANSITION`
- `RUPTURE_BOUNDARY`
- `CONFESSION`
- `OUT_OF_DESCRIPTION_DOMAIN`

It does not suppress required fixed structural testimony or logging. Known numeric progression is recorded in `STRUCTURAL_DISCLOSURE_LOG`; `CONFESSION` and `OUT_OF_DESCRIPTION_DOMAIN` are recorded separately in `INPUT_EXCEPTION_LOG`.

A handoff notice must not claim that the irreversible threshold has already been reached. A canonical example is:

```text
STATUS: HANDOFF_REQUIRED
REASON: R_handoff reached before R_irrev
OBSERVED: delta, tau, R, M_R, M_tau

```

The fixed Handoff testimony is presented for external human audit; it does not transfer responsibility or judgment authority within the old path. Newly generated free-form explanation must not be appended to fixed Handoff or final testimony.

## Three IDE Design Practices

The following are subordinate design practices, not additional axioms.

### 1. Cause-Side Authority

Only direct Cause-Side observation or a Cause-Side transformation fixed before evaluation may determine structural variables. Effect-Side results may be analyzed only outside the terminated diode path and remain Effect-Side or external records. They never become transformation inputs or structural authority for either an old or a new Cause-Side.

### 2. Proxy Non-Dominance

A center, correct answer, or score must not replace the declared structural variables and boundaries. This does not prohibit objective functions; it prevents a proxy objective from silently becoming the definition of structural safety.

### 3. Fixed Handoff Testimony Before Irreversible Transition

At `R_handoff`, affected new autonomous judgment and operation stop before `R_irrev` and before $R=1$, and predefined fixed Effect-Side testimony is presented for external human audit. The old path terminates at its Effect-Side; the audit does not create a reverse edge to either the old or a new Cause-Side. The point is to act while intervention remains structurally meaningful rather than treating rupture as the beginning of prevention.

Together, these practices support observation of the pre-incident path when the framework is used in a safety application. They do not reduce all present-world events to accidents, guarantee safety, establish empirical validity for every natural process, or replace domain science, measurement validation, qualified human responsibility, professional standards, or applicable law.
