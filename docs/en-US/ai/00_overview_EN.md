## The Canonical Starting Point

NRA-IDE begins from the single axiom:

> **Existence is Generation.**

The axiom is not an operating threshold, safety rule, or additional formula. “Strictness without play collapses” is a structural persistence principle, not a second axiom.

The Primary Formula expresses the state of a declared target as a genuine mathematical root formula:

```text
R = delta / tau
```

It must not be reduced to a safety indicator, local instrument, or mere boundary-approach rate. The Secondary / Dual-Fluctuation Formula is the IDE computational-method and dynamics engine. Other equations are complementary, auxiliary, or derived equations; they are not axioms.

## The Survival Domain and Its Safety-Oriented Application

NRA-IDE itself is the survival equation and survival domain, implemented through IDE as a computational-method and dynamics engine. The safety domain is the partial domain that applies the survival domain to accident prevention, operation, and control. It is not the whole survival domain and does not guarantee safety.

Survival is not permanent preservation of the same form. It includes dynamic persistence in which fluctuation, phase transition, rupture, selection, disappearance, and reconstruction generate new structures and histories.

The safety-oriented chapters therefore ask how a declared Cause-Side trajectory can be observed and handled before irreversible transition or rupture. External human audit may inspect fixed Effect-Side testimony, but it does not inherit judgment authority inside the old causal path.

## Canonical State Map

The three canonical operating thresholds have the invariant order:

```text
0 <= R_warn < R_handoff < R_irrev < 1
```

The normal numeric progression is:

```text
PERMIT
→ BOUNDARY_WARNING
→ HANDOFF_REQUIRED
→ IRREVERSIBLE_TRANSITION
→ RUPTURE_BOUNDARY
```

`CONFESSION` and `OUT_OF_DESCRIPTION_DOMAIN` handle unsupported or undefined evaluations separately.

- At `R_warn`, boundary approach is disclosed.
- At `R_handoff`, affected new autonomous judgment and operation stop, and predefined fixed Effect-Side testimony is presented for external human audit.
- At `R_irrev`, irreversible transition is latched. Within the same history, later $R$ decrease, automatic processing, manual intervention, human review, approval, or version update cannot release the latch.
- At $R\ge1$, the declared target reports `RUPTURE_BOUNDARY` and switches to post-rupture fixed testimony. Surviving observation, logging, and communication channels continue independently until each becomes unavailable.

The threshold values and applicable state conditions are established by declared Cause-Side domain authority before evaluation. An Effect-Side result, including a low reported $R$, cannot authorize widening $\tau$, redefining $\delta$, changing a threshold, or rewriting a state condition.

## Observation Is Not the Same as Quantification

In a nonlinear phase-transition region, numerical precision may decrease while physical signs in the process, path, and approach to an irreversible threshold remain observable. Sensor saturation, widening uncertainty, model weakness, or limited observation time does not justify invented precision, and lack of exact quantification does not by itself prove lack of observation.

NRA-IDE keeps separate:

- the observed Cause-Side sign;
- measured value and unit;
- uncertainty and instrument limits;
- the pre-fixed transformation rule;
- the resulting canonical state;
- unavailable information.

When required evidence is absent, the system reports the applicable exception state instead of filling the gap from Effect-Side material.

## The Causal Diode and History Boundary

The Causal Diode is not merely a prohibition on backward inference. It is a complete one-way authority boundary:

```text
Old Cause-Side
→ old Causal Diode
→ Old Effect-Side
→ TERMINAL
```

Old Effect-Side values, canonical thresholds, states, the irreversible latch, rules, transformation inputs, update grounds, or provenance must not be connected back to either the old or a new Cause-Side. This prohibition applies to automatic processing, manual intervention, human review, approval, version updates, incident analysis, and future rule preparation.

If a later evaluation is needed, it begins as an independent history:

```text
independently declared new target
→ newly established Cause-Side observations and pre-fixed rules
→ new Causal Diode
→ New Effect-Side
```

Old Effect-Side material must not be imported, relabeled, reconstructed, or reused as Cause-Side authority. Physical remnants may be observed anew as part of an independently declared target, but that new observation is not a transfer of old Effect-Side values or authority.

External human audit, incident analysis, and preparation of future rules occur outside the terminated old Causal Diode. They do not continue or rewrite it.

## Fail-Closed and Testimony

Fail-Closed suppresses affected new autonomous judgment and operation for `HANDOFF_REQUIRED`, `IRREVERSIBLE_TRANSITION`, `RUPTURE_BOUNDARY`, `CONFESSION`, and `OUT_OF_DESCRIPTION_DOMAIN`. It does not suppress required fixed structural testimony or logging. `PERMIT` is not Fail-Closed, and `BOUNDARY_WARNING` alone does not require complete output suppression unless a pre-fixed domain rule requires it.

Logs and fixed reports are terminal Effect-Side or external testimony. They may be inspected, but their values, decisions, canonical thresholds, states, the irreversible latch, rules, transformation inputs, update grounds, or provenance cannot become Cause-Side authority through calculation, manual review, approval, relabeling, reconstruction, reuse, or version update.

Ordinary explanation is permitted only in states and fields allowed by pre-fixed canonical behavior. Newly generated free-form explanation is not appended to fixed Handoff or final testimony.

## Structural Invariants

The following boundaries remain unchanged throughout the chapters:

* **One Axiom**: “Existence is Generation.” There is no second axiom.
* **Formula Classification**: The Primary Formula and Secondary / Dual-Fluctuation Formula are IDE calculation systems, not axioms; supplementary equations remain subordinate.
* **Survival Before Safety Reduction**: The survival equation and survival domain are the body of NRA-IDE; the safety domain is a partial operational application and not a guarantee.
* **Observation Without Invented Precision**: Quantification failure and observation failure are not identical.
* **Locality of Linear Approximation**: Linear calculation applies where a locally static approximation is valid and is not generalized to nature as a whole.
* **Canonical Thresholds and States**: Threshold order, state behavior, and the irreversible latch cannot be rewritten from Effect-Side results.
* **Complete One-Way Causal Diode**: Cause-Side may produce Effect-Side testimony; Effect-Side never becomes old or new Cause-Side authority.
* **Old Path Terminates**: Later evaluation starts from an independently declared target, new Cause-Side observations and rules, and a new Causal Diode.
* **Conditional LLM Configuration**: An LLM sandwich is a configuration, not the definition of NRA-IDE or proof of conformance.
* **Fail-Closed With Fixed Testimony**: Affected autonomous operation stops while required fixed testimony and logs remain available for external audit.
