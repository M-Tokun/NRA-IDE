# 03 Causal Diode — Enforcing the One-Way Path from Cause-Side to Effect-Side

> The Causal Diode is a subordinate authority-separation design practice. It does not guarantee safety, measurement validity, causal correctness, output correctness, or suitability in a particular domain.

## Where Backward Inference Becomes a Problem

Within a Causal Diode, no inference, information, artifact, judgment, or authority travels from Effect-Side back to Cause-Side. The permitted direction is Cause-Side to Effect-Side only.

Research, incident investigation, scientific explanation, human review, and future rule design may occur only outside the completed diode path. They do not continue that path and do not turn an old Effect-Side artifact into either an old or a new Cause-Side input.

NRA-IDE therefore blocks every Effect-Side-to-Cause-Side return path, whether automatic, manual, reviewed, authorized, or versioned.

For example, a model must not reduce accumulated deviation $\delta$ or enlarge absorption thickness $\tau$ merely because its previous output received a favorable semantic score.

```text
generated result
→ favorable Effect-Side evaluation
→ automatic rewrite of delta or tau
→ next canonical state appears safer
```

This path replaces observed provenance with a result-dependent estimate. The structural basis can no longer be traced independently from the output it was meant to evaluate.

Any attempted reverse connection is called $\Pi^{-1}$ in this explanatory chapter.

---

## The Diode Has No Reverse Path

An electrical diode motivates the one-way metaphor. In the Causal Diode, the path itself is absolute: Cause-Side to Effect-Side only, with no reverse edge.

The runtime authority direction is:

```text
Cause-Side observation or pre-fixed transformation
→ determination of delta and tau
→ calculation of canonical R
→ canonical state and fixed testimony
→ Effect-Side explanation or generation
```

After this path terminates, Effect-Side artifacts may be inspected, compared, audited, or investigated only as an external activity. They remain Effect-Side or external records and never acquire authority to rewrite $R$, $\delta$, $\tau$, canonical thresholds, canonical states, an irreversible latch, rules, transformation inputs, update grounds, or provenance in the old history or in a new history.

Even if an LLM produces the conclusion “SAFE,” the label cannot reduce $\delta$, enlarge $\tau$, clear `irreversible_latched`, or change a canonical state.

## How to Distinguish Cause-Side from Effect-Side

The distinction is based on provenance, observation procedure, transformation rules, and update authority—not on a value's name alone.

### Cause-Side structural inputs

- **$\delta$ — accumulated deviation:** obtained from direct observation or a transformation rule fixed before evaluation.
- **$\tau$ — absorption thickness:** obtained from direct observation or a pre-fixed domain model supported by Cause-Side history. It is not a time constant.
- **Thresholds:** `R_warn`, `R_handoff`, and `R_irrev`, with target, units where applicable, evidence, uncertainty, version, and update authority fixed before evaluation.
- **Domain observations:** physical position, distance, load, temperature, or another quantity may be Cause-Side when its observation and transformation path are valid for the declared target.

$R$ is a canonical derived value, not a raw Cause-Side input:

$$
R=\frac{\delta}{\tau}
$$

### Effect-Side artifacts

- generated text or images;
- model self-evaluation;
- semantic labels, similarity scores, rankings, and proxy objectives;
- selected, rejected, or discarded generated outputs;
- coordinates, centers, or distances produced by a model or evaluator;
- $\delta$ or $\tau$ reconstructed only after inspecting a generated result.

A physical distance measured by a qualified sensor is not prohibited merely because it is called “distance.” A model-generated distance does not become Cause-Side merely because it uses the same unit. Provenance and the pre-fixed transformation rule decide the role.

## Three Different Histories and Logs

The word “log” must not collapse three different authorities.

### 1. Cause-Side observation history

Measured load, position, temperature, damage, or other declared observations may contribute to later $\delta$ or $\tau$ only through a transformation or update rule fixed before the evaluation. This is input history with Cause-Side provenance.

### 2. Canonical NRA-IDE logs

- Known numeric structural progression is recorded in `STRUCTURAL_DISCLOSURE_LOG`.
- `CONFESSION` and `OUT_OF_DESCRIPTION_DOMAIN` are recorded separately in `INPUT_EXCEPTION_LOG`.

These logs preserve testimony. Their existence creates no return path and grants no rule for changing later structural variables. Any new Cause-Side history must begin independently.

### 3. Effect-Side generation and discard records

Generated, selected, rejected, or discarded outputs remain Effect-Side audit records. They must not be mixed into the two canonical structural logs as though they were Cause-Side measurement.

## The Moment Prohibited $\Pi^{-1}$ Arises

$\Pi^{-1}$ arises whenever an Effect-Side artifact is connected back to Cause-Side, including through manual review, authorization, versioning, or a later evaluation.

```text
Effect-Side output or score
→ result-dependent estimate of delta or tau
→ automatic structural update
→ next canonical evaluation
```

An example is: “The previous output was judged safe, therefore current $\tau$ should be expanded.” The judgment is Effect-Side; it cannot establish physical replenishment or a validated increase in absorption thickness.

The allowed runtime path is:

```text
Cause-Side observation and pre-fixed rule
→ delta and tau
→ R and canonical state
→ Effect-Side processing
```

## Human Investigation and Future Rule Revision Are Outside the Old Diode

The old causal path ends at its Effect-Side:

```text
Old Cause-Side
→ old Causal Diode
→ Old Effect-Side
→ TERMINAL
```

Post-incident investigation, human interpretation, and future rule design may occur outside that terminated path. An investigator may read the old records, but those records retain their Effect-Side or external provenance. They are not converted into an old Cause-Side input, a new Cause-Side input, or structural evidence for either history.

A later evaluation must start as an independent history:

```text
independently declared new target
→ new Cause-Side observations, history, and pre-fixed rules
→ new Causal Diode
→ New Effect-Side
```

There is no arrow from Old Effect-Side to either Cause-Side. A future rule is designed and authorized outside the old diode, then fixed before the new evaluation begins. Its design record may cite the prior incident, but no old Effect-Side value is imported, relabeled, reconstructed, or treated as a Cause-Side measurement, structural variable, or provenance.

The new description must declare its target, observations, evidence, uncertainty, applicability, version, and update authority independently. It does not retroactively change the testimony or structural state of the completed evaluation.

After rupture or disappearance of a declared target, a human may declare a new target structure and a new history. That history starts from its own Cause-Side and its own Causal Diode; it is not a restoration, continuation, or rewrite of the former structure.

## What the Causal Diode Protects

The Causal Diode protects the independence and traceability of structural evaluation:

1. A proxy score cannot silently become the structural definition it is intended to assess.
2. LLM output cannot rewrite $R$, $\delta$, $\tau$, canonical thresholds, canonical states, an irreversible latch, rules, transformation inputs, update grounds, or provenance.
3. Known structural testimony remains distinguishable from input exceptions and generated-output records.
4. Later human learning remains external to the terminated path and cannot return old Effect-Side material to either Cause-Side.

The Causal Diode does not establish that Cause-Side measurements are correct, that the transformation rule is valid, or that a domain model is complete. Those require independent measurement and domain validation.

---

## Application Beyond Accident Analysis

The authority boundary applies to the wider NRA-IDE scope of generation, change, accumulation, phase transition, rupture, disappearance, and new history. Accident prevention is one narrow application.

In any application, a later interpretation cannot recreate the earlier target's structural inputs or seed a new Cause-Side. A new description begins from independently declared Cause-Side observations, history, and pre-fixed rules under a new Causal Diode. It does not erase, reverse, continue, or convert the provenance of the prior history.
