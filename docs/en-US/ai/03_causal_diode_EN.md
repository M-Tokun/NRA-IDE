# 03 Causal Diode — Structurally Blocking Feedback from the Effect-Side to the Cause-Side

<!-- FILE: 03_causal_diode_EN.md -->

---

## Where Backward Inference Becomes a Problem

Reasoning from effects back to causes is not itself prohibited in research or explanatory contexts.

What NRA-IDE blocks is the path that returns values generated or evaluated on the **Effect-Side** to the Cause-Side inputs of the next structural evaluation.

For example, this occurs when scores or evaluation values are used to update the next $\delta$ or $\tau$ on the assumption that “this value will make the system appear safe.”

Here, the result recreates the Cause-Side values. The provenance of the observed values is replaced by the Effect-Side, and the basis for structural evaluation can no longer be traced.

This path is called $\Pi^{-1}$.

The Causal Diode is not a mechanism for eliminating every error or semantic uncertainty inside AI. It is a mechanism that prevents the inputs of the structural safety gate from being rewritten by Effect-Side values.

---

## The Diode Concept

A diode in an electrical circuit allows current to flow in only one direction.

Even when voltage is applied in the reverse direction, current does not flow. It does not merely require caution against backflow; it establishes a structure in which backflow cannot occur.

The Causal Diode operates in the same way.

Information may flow from the Cause-Side to the Effect-Side. It does not flow from the Effect-Side to the Cause-Side.

Even when AI produces a conclusion such as “SAFE,” that conclusion cannot be used as a reason to reduce $\delta$ or enlarge $\tau$.

Structural state is determined only by Cause-Side observation and update rules defined in advance.

---

## How to Distinguish the Cause-Side from the Effect-Side

The distinction here is not the name of a value, but **its provenance and update path**.

Values that may be placed on the Cause-Side are those derived from the state or load of the target system and traceable through observation procedures or update rules defined in advance.

* **$\delta$ (fluctuation):** A deviation occurring in the target system. Its measurement method is determined in advance, and it is received as an observation.
* **$\tau$ (structural thickness):** The structural tolerance defined at design time and its current value, updated from Cause-Side load history in accordance with that rule. $\tau$ is not necessarily a raw sensor value, but it must not be updated by Effect-Side outputs or evaluations.
* **$\omega$ (angular velocity), $C$ (constraint), and accumulated deviation:** Values derived from the movement or load of the target system whose provenance can be verified.

By contrast, $R$ is not an input received from the Cause-Side. It is a **structural-state ratio** calculated from Cause-Side $\delta$ and $\tau$ as $R = \delta / \tau$.

Values placed on the Effect-Side are those obtained through model generation, evaluation, or interpretation.

* Evaluation scores, similarity measures, and rankings
* Distances, coordinates, or centers generated by models or evaluators
* Semantic labels, previous generated text, and log contents
* $\delta$ or $\tau$ re-estimated after examining results

The distances and coordinates referred to here are values generated as the result of evaluation, inference, or projection. This does not mean that physical positions or distances directly obtained by sensors are prohibited uniformly. Whether a value may be passed to the structural gate is determined not by its name, but by its observation procedure and generation process.

---

## The Moment $\Pi^{-1}$ Arises

$\Pi^{-1}$ arises when an Effect-Side value is used to update a Cause-Side input of the structural gate.

```text
Effect-Side log → Cause-Side estimation → update of δ or τ → next structural evaluation
```

For example, suppose a past output is reviewed and the system concludes, “The previous output was evaluated as safe, so the current $\tau$ should be expanded.” In that case, the log becomes the basis for updating $\tau$.

In this path, an Effect-Side interpretation, rather than Cause-Side load history, changes the structural state.

The Causal Diode does not permit this path.

```text
Cause-Side observations and load history → determination of δ and τ → calculation of R → Effect-Side processing
```

Effect-Side processing may refer to Cause-Side values. However, its results cannot return to the inputs or update grounds of $\delta$, $\tau$, or $R$.

---

## What Is Protected by Blocking Backflow?

The Causal Diode protects the basis of structural evaluation.

First, it prevents the decision values of the safety gate from being directly fed back as scores for the model to optimize. It does not eliminate every form of external optimization, but it blocks the path through which NRA-IDE’s structural evaluation itself is recreated by Effect-Side evaluation values.

Second, it prevents LLM outputs and evaluation results from rewriting $\tau$. $\tau$ may change only through rules fixed at design time and the physical or observational history of the Cause-Side.

Third, it makes it possible to preserve logs as testimony. Logs record what occurred, but they are not used as inputs to the next structural evaluation. By separating records from computation, the basis for stopping or delegation can be verified afterward.

The important point is that the Causal Diode does not guarantee the correctness of output content. The possibility that an LLM may produce semantic errors, and the quality of output, are separate issues.

What the Causal Diode guarantees is that Effect-Side values do not alter Cause-Side structural evaluation.

---

## What Is Examined in Boundary Exploration

To determine whether a system remains within a structural boundary or is approaching an irreversible regime, quantities with verifiable Cause-Side provenance are used.

What deviations have been observed? What load history has changed the structural thickness? Is the structure continuing to transition?

Semantic evaluation, scores, and previous outputs do not serve as grounds for this determination.

By preventing Effect-Side values from returning as inputs, the starting point of structural evaluation remains fixed on the observable side.
