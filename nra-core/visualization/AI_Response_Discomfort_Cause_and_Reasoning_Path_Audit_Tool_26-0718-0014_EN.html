5. Surface scan — an entry point, not a cause determination

No detection does not mean the reasoning path is sound. Use the generated prompt to have the AI decompose implicit premise changes, semantic differences, and evidence misconnections.

6. Audit prompts to generate

① ??? Cause Exploration Without presupposing the cause, explore up to three candidates involving claim differences, added premises, or missing links.
② Detailed Reasoning Path Audit Assign IDs to claims, evidence, intermediate judgments, and conclusions, then inspect each connection.
③ Detailed Evidence / False-Denial Audit Examine search non-findings, general statements, scope mismatches, and whether direct refutation is established.
④ Return to the Original Issue After the audit, return to the original question without mixing established and unresolved points.

Normally, begin with ① and proceed to ② or ③ after candidate causes emerge. You do not need to submit all four prompts at once.
