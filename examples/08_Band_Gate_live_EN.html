WHY ASYMMETRIC?

Upper: EMA pressure accumulates → τ widens → peak rounded (absorbs shock).
Lower: EMA drain accumulates → τ narrows → dropout caught early (sensitive).
Identical to heart valves, car suspension, and pressure-relief valves.
