This system is constructed independently from the “water → ice (freezing)” equation. Because the tracked reference structure changes from “liquid” to “solid (crystal lattice),” δ and τ are defined separately. Reverse-calculating this equation from the freezing formula is prohibited by the NRA-IDE design principle.

Definition of the Calculation Formula
Static τ Model (Linear Type)
δ = T − (−20) = T + 20 → accumulated deviation from the ice reference temperature (−20°C)
τ = 20 → fixed constant (total margin that the crystal lattice structure can maintain)
R = δ / τ = (T + 20) / 20
R = 1.0 exactly coincides with T = 0°C. The graph is linear.
Move the temperature slider to update δ, τ, and R in real time. At R ≥ 1.0, the system enters Fail-Closed (the structure is confirmed as water). At R ≥ 0.75, it enters the warning zone (the stage where the crystal structure begins to relax).

Fundamental Difference from the Freezing System: In the freezing system, δ is “deviation from the liquid structure (temperature decrease).” In the melting system, δ is “deviation from the solid crystal structure (temperature increase).” Even at the same 0°C point, the tracked structure is different, so this system is designed independently.
