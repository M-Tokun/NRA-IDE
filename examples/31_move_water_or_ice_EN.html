Selected Axiom System (Calculation Formula)
[Independence of the Nomological Ring Axioms]
Water and ice are physically the same H₂O, but in NRA-IDE the “reference structure being tracked” is different.
The freezing system measures entropy reduction from the water reference state (20℃), while the melting system measures heat accumulation from the ice reference state (-20℃).
The two formulas must not be reverse-calculated or substituted into each other. This separation is a core safety design principle of the framework: Fail-Closed.
Assumptions and limits of this screen (caveats)
• The specific heats (water 4.18, ice 2.09 kJ/(kg·K)) and the heat of fusion 334 kJ/kg are standard values for 1 kg of pure water. Supercooling, impurities, and pressure are not modeled.
• In the dynamic τ model, R ≥ 1.0 is a model-side alert boundary (T = 10°C when freezing, −10°C when melting), not the physical phase transition (0°C).
