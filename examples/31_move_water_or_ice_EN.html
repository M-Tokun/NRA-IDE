Water and ice are physically the same H₂O, but in NRA-IDE the “reference structure being tracked” is different.
The freezing system measures entropy reduction from the water reference state (20℃), while the melting system measures heat accumulation from the ice reference state (-20℃).
The two formulas must not be reverse-calculated or substituted into each other. This separation is a core safety design principle of the framework: Fail-Closed.
