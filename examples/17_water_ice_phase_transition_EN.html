Formula Definitions
Static τ Model (linear)
δ = 20 − T → accumulated deviation from the liquid reference temperature (20°C)
τ = 20 → fixed constant, the total margin held by the liquid structure
R = δ / τ = (20 − T) / 20
R = 1.0 exactly coincides with T = 0°C. The graph is linear.
Move the temperature slider to update δ, τ, and R in real time. R ≥ 1.0 triggers Fail-Closed, where the structure is determined as ice. R ≥ 0.75 indicates the warning zone.
Assumptions and limits of this screen (caveats)
• The specific heats (water 4.18, ice 2.09 kJ/(kg·K)) and the heat of fusion 334 kJ/kg are standard values for 1 kg of pure water. Supercooling, impurities, and pressure are not modeled.
• In the dynamic τ model, R ≥ 1.0 is a model-side alert boundary (T = 10°C when freezing, −10°C when melting), not the physical phase transition (0°C).
