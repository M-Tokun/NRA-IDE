Assumptions and limits of this screen (caveats)
• The reference values of MGF, PI, and DF and the thicknesses from reference to limit (MGF 15, PI threshold − 2.8, DF 30) are demo-declared values, not clinical criteria. Do not use this for diagnosis or treatment decisions.
• R is the maximum of the per-channel R_i = δ_i/τ_i of the three quantities (no summing, no averaging).
• The PI-upper and MGF-lower sliders change the declaration of the boundary, so moving them starts a new evaluation (the latch is released and the arrival record is stored).
• Body temperature is used only to narrow the thickness (a higher temperature narrows it; a lower one does not widen it).
• The DF reference (DF_REF) is declared as the value at monitoring start.
