🔴 Root Cause of System Failure

Conventional: Triple Trap

  1. Linear Approximation: Linearizing non-linear reality → Continuous small offsets.
  2. Averaging: Crushing individual data into means → Loss of critical edge cases.
  3. Floating-Point: IEEE 754 precision issues (0.1+0.1+0.1 ≠ 0.3) → Rounding drift.

Outcome: Exponential Error Growth
1,000 steps: ~10% Error
10,000 steps: ~200% Error
100,000 steps: Total Collapse

NRA-IDE: The Solution

  1. Non-linear Retention: Preserves structural integrity → No approximation needed.
  2. No Averaging: Directly measures constraint deviation (δ) → Zero information loss.
  3. Integer Phase Lock: Logic based on integer phases → Eliminates precision drift.

Outcome: Absolute Stability
Steady at 0% error regardless of step count.
