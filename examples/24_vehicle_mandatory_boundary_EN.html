NRA-IDE mandatory boundary + physical metrics demo

Boundary Monitoring Demo for Mandatory Autonomous Driving Limits, with Additional Physical Monitoring Quantities Shown in Parallel

The three mandatory subsystems are kept intact, while forward obstacle distance, relative speed, yaw-rate error, lane confidence, and stopping time margin are added separately as independent monitoring quantities. This is not a replacement, but a parallel arrangement. In addition, a danger-zone slider and DANGER indicator are included as examples so that threshold crossings can be recognized at a glance.
Mandatory limits = three subsystems: external-world recognition, distance/velocity, and ego-motion
Additional monitoring = distance, relative speed, yaw-rate error, lane confidence, and stopping time margin
No internal mixing / connection allowed = IDE is an external boundary and connects to the legacy stack only through an adapter

Design policy
1. Treat camera, radar, and ego-motion as the three mandatory limits.
2. The five physical quantities are monitored separately, not as replacements.
3. The three subsystems interact, but are not compressed into a single truth value.
4. Place the IDE outside the existing control stack and let it receive input connections only.
5. Separate the outputs into PASS / DAMP / FALLBACK and DANGER display.

Scenario controls

Deliberately create incompatible observations and deteriorating physical monitoring quantities, and observe how the IDE behaves outside the legacy stack.
