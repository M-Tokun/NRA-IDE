# Ethical Statement

### This document describes the ethical position of the Nomological Ring Axioms (NRA) project and the Intensional Dynamics Engine (IDE) framework.

There is exactly one Nomological Ring Axiom: “Existence is Generation.” No second or subsequent axiom exists. “Nomological Ring Axioms” remains the proper project name abbreviated as NRA.

The Intensional Dynamics Engine (IDE) is a structural evaluation framework under this theory. Its Primary and Secondary / Dual-Fluctuation Formulas are the two canonical IDE calculation systems, not axioms. Other equations are derived, auxiliary, or complementary.

This ethical statement does not create or modify an axiom, canonical definition, boundary state, formula classification, implementation requirement, or canonical precedence.

The author strongly opposes, and asks that this theoretical framework and its derived implementations not be used for:


- criminal activities

- military use

- human rights violations

The purpose of NRA-IDE is to support safety-oriented understanding and evaluation of structural states in complex systems.

NRA-IDE does not independently guarantee safety, fitness for a particular purpose, legal or regulatory compliance, measurement validity, or effective operation in any domain. Repository tests establish only the behavior they explicitly test; they do not validate a medical, military, infrastructure, engineering, or other high-risk application.

In domains involving human life, health, rights, physical safety, critical infrastructure, or irreversible consequences, NRA-IDE must not replace qualified human responsibility, independent domain validation, applicable professional standards, or required legal and regulatory review.

This statement represents the ethical position of the author and a strong non-binding request to users. It is not an additional software-license restriction.

It does not replace, narrow, or override the MIT License, which governs the legal permissions for the software and documents in this repository. Creating legally binding use restrictions would require a separate licensing decision and is not accomplished by this statement.

Users remain responsible for how they apply the theory and its implementations.
