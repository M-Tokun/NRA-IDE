# Ethical Statement

### This document describes the ethical position of the Nomological Ring Axioms (NRA) project and the Intensional Dynamics Engine (IDE) framework.
The Nomological Ring Axioms define a theoretical framework describing existence, generation, state transition, and structural boundaries.
The Intensional Dynamics Engine (IDE) is a structural evaluation framework derived from this theory.
This project does not permit the use of this theoretical framework or its derived implementations for:

- criminal activities

- military use

- human rights violations

The purpose of NRA-IDE is to support the safe understanding and evaluation of structural states in complex systems.
This statement represents the ethical position of the author.
It does not replace or override the MIT License, which governs the legal distribution of the software and documents in this repository.

Users remain responsible for how they apply the theory and its implementations.
