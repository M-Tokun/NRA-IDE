## Authorship



The logical structures, axioms, and algorithms of the Nomological Ring Axioms and the Intensional Dynamics Engine were built by M‑Tokuni through personal thought and logical reasoning. They were not generated by automated generation technologies. They are a theoretical framework formed through a deliberate process of conceptual design.



---



## On Derivatives



The MIT License permits commercial use of derivatives. This is not in conflict with the developer's intent. There is, however, one personal wish the developer would like to put on record.



I do not want derivatives of this framework to be closed off for the exclusive benefit of any particular individual, company, or organization.



My honest hope is that useful technology reaches as many people as possible, as quickly as possible. I ask that the core logic and axioms not be isolated from others through patents. Even in commercial use, I hope that consideration will be given to ensuring the benefits of this technology reach not only people living today, but future generations as well.



Unless a situation arises in which this framework is misused or causes clear harm to humanity, I have no intention of filing patents myself.



---



## On Humanitarian Use



The design principle of this framework is to secure safety through structural integrity. In domains directly connected to human life — medicine, life support, social infrastructure, safety engineering — I hope this framework will be used without technical or economic barriers.



---



## On Research and Development



Research, theoretical extension, verification, and refutation based on this framework are all welcome. This framework is not a finished doctrine. It is offered as an open foundation for inquiry. I hope that knowledge gained from it will be returned to the world in as open a form as possible.

## 派生物について



MIT ライセンスは派生物の商業利用を許容します。それ自体は開発者の意図と矛盾しません。ただ、一点だけ個人的な願いを記しておきます。



本フレームワークから派生したものが、特定の誰かの独占的利益のために閉じられることを望みません。



有用な技術は、できるだけ早く、できるだけ広く届いてほしいというのが率直な気持ちです。核心的な論理や公理が特許によって他者から切り離される事態は避けてほしいと思います。商業利用においても、その技術の恩恵が現在の人だけでなく、将来の世代にも届くよう配慮していただけると幸いです。



なお、悪用される局面や人類に明らかな不利益をもたらす事態が生じない限り、私自身が特許申請を行う予定はありません。
